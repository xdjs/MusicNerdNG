Reality notes. The app mostly reaches for .glass panels, not the stock Card — so two padding conventions coexist (Card's rigid p-6 vs. glass p-4/p-5) and two radii (8px token vs. glass 16px literal). The .pink-btn declares font-family:"KoHo", but KoHo is never actually loaded in the app, so all text here renders in the real system sans stack (-apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto) — shown faithfully. Semantic colors are unmodified shadcn "slate"; the brand's candy-neon pink/blue/green live outside the tokens as hex literals.
