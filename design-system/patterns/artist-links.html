Artist links grid

The signature MusicNerd platform-link renderer: circular frosted-glass tiles that lift and glow on hover — the "pink-glow lift" gesture. Two renderers coexist in the app; the glass grid is the newer, signature one.

Reality note: labels are declared font-family:"KoHo" in globals.css, but the webfont never loads — so text renders in the system sans stack, shown here. Glow uses #ef95ff rgba(239,149,255,.45), not the #ff9ce3 title glow (two pink hexes coexist).
