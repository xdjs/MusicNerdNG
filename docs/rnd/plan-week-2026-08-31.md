## The ask cannot answer a question about "lately"

This is the real one, and it is bigger than the missing citations.

The ask **never reads the artist's posts**. It reads only the credits and
statements extracted from them. And `posted_at` is stored on every one of those
rows but `getSocialCredits` drops it on the way out, so even the derived
material arrives with no dates attached.

So "what has Pete Rango been up to lately" is structurally unanswerable: 299
posts available, none read, and nothing that is read can be ordered by time.

Fix:

- Carry `postedAt` through `getSocialCredits` and into the prompt.
- Give the ask the recent posts themselves — captions, dates, links — not just
  what was extracted from them months of feed ago. Recency questions want the
  post, not the credit.
- Order what it sees by date so "lately" means something.

**On fetching live from Instagram per question:** not viable. A scrape is one
to five minutes and the ask is a text box someone is waiting at. Freshness
comes from the stored feed being refreshed — the "Look again" button and the
job queue already do that, and the artist can trigger it. If the posts are
stale, that is a refresh problem, not an ask problem.

**Done when** the meeting's own test — "what has Pete Rango been up to lately"
— answers with something actually recent, from a post, with a link to it.

## Domain credibility list

Boom Play and Clubhouse, in the shape Carl and CY described: a list the judge
consults while deciding, not an ordering applied afterwards. What exists today
only sorts.

- Three tiers: preferred, unknown, low-signal.
- The tier reaches `judgeSourceRelevance`, so a low-signal domain needs
  stronger evidence to survive.
- One editable list, so adding a domain is a one-line change.

It ranks and gates. A low-signal source that survives still reaches the artist,
who decides.

**Amended 27 August.** Pete, on the Boomplay page in his own vault: "I don't
want boom play anywhere... I don't want any links like that." So there is a
fourth category above the three tiers, and it does delete: a short explicit list
of hosts that never enter the vault at all. The line is authorship, not quality.
Every page on boomplay.com, viberate.com, soundcharts.com and clubhousedb.com is
generated from a scrape or a catalogue feed, so there is no article to read and
nothing the artist said. Ranking such a page low still leaves it on their page.

**Done when** a fresh run on Pete Rango files no Boom Play and no Clubhouse,
and the benchmark still scores 10/12 with zero wrong.

## Bot scrapability

Start with the measurement, because it decides whether this is an afternoon or
a project: fetch an artist page the way a bot does, JavaScript disabled, and
see what is actually in the HTML.

Then: server-render the profile content that matters, publish the knowledge
base as a crawlable page, add structured data so a crawler knows what it is
looking at.

The public knowledge base is **for LLMs and search engines**. Fans need a
better surface than the edit view — separate work, not now.

Not doing yet: making the MCP server public without an auth token. Carl said
track it, not do it.

**Done when** an artist page returns its substance in the initial HTML and the
knowledge base is readable without a login.
