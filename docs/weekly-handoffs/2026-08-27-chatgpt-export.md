## Carry-forward context from Pete's latest corrections

The following points are included only so Claude does not mistake previously established ideas for new discoveries if they surface in adjacent context:

- Music Nerd's role in creating curiosity around a song or artist is already established. Pete does not want this repeatedly reframed as a new strategic question.
- Music Nerd already intends to turn listening into participation, exploration, support, contribution, and other actions. This should not be presented as a newly discovered direction.
- The team already understands scenes as networks of relationships rather than primarily genre or geography. The live product question is how those relationships are represented, displayed, and made explorable.
- A more useful product question is what applications can be built on top of the Music Nerd database to let people explore relational music data, and what interfaces make those relationships meaningful rather than producing a generic network graph.
- For Music Nerd Roundtable / Showcase concepts, Pete is interested not only in the gathering itself but in the stories, relationships, and source material that can emerge because Music Nerd hosts and records these spaces. Events can create conditions for knowledge and connections to become visible that would not necessarily be discoverable through scraping or existing APIs.

Pete specifically corrected an interpretation that reduced the relevance of the reference to Music Nerd becoming a "school." The more relevant question is what stories and knowledge emerge from convening people and how Music Nerd can preserve and make that material explorable afterward.
